Fermi Quiz

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

In short: Fermi Quiz does not collect, store or share any personal data.

What data we process

None. The app has no advertising, analytics or tracking, and it needs no account to play. We have no servers that receive data from you.

Your progress

Your answers, statistics and preferences are saved only on your device. No one else has access to them, and they are deleted if you delete the app.

Reminders

If you turn on reminders, they are scheduled as local notifications on your iPhone. They never leave your device, and you can turn them off at any time.

Sharing results

If you share a result, you do it with the iOS share sheet and you choose where it goes. Fermi Quiz does not send anything on its own. The link to challenge a friend contains only the challenge number and your score.

Game Center (optional)

Leaderboards are off by default. If you turn them on in Settings, your result for the day and your best streak are sent to Game Center, an Apple service, linked to your Game Center account, so they can appear on the leaderboards. That processing is carried out by Apple under its own privacy policy; we do not receive or store that data. You can turn it off at any time.

Web version

The browser version (fermi.getmindmap.com/reto) saves your progress only in your browser's local storage. It uses no cookies, analytics or advertising. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web server, may log technical details of your visit, such as your IP address.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page. If you have any questions, write to us here.
