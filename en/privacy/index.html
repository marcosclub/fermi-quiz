Fermi Quiz

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

In short: Fermi Quiz does not collect, store or share any personal data.

What data we process

None. The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking, and it does not connect to any servers, whether our own or third-party.

Your progress

Your answers, statistics and preferences are saved only on your device. No one else has access to them, and they are deleted if you delete the app.

Reminders

If you turn on reminders, they are scheduled as local notifications on your iPhone. They never leave your device, and you can turn them off at any time.

Sharing results

If you share a result, you do it with the iOS share sheet and you choose where it goes. Fermi Quiz does not send anything on its own.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page. If you have any questions, write to us here.

Data controller: The Faculty Club SL.
